A FEW RULES FOR SURVIVAL

TIME IS
IN YOUR HANDS.

Stay still to slow enemies and bullets. Move to speed up the world. In co-op, the more active player sets the pace.

QUEST 3

Grip: hold to grab, release to throw.
Trigger: fire a held pistol.
Left stick: move.
Right stick: snap turn.
Trigger with empty hands: start or restart between runs.

DESKTOP

WASD: move. Mouse: aim.
E: grab / drop nearby object.
Q: throw forward.
Click: fire. Esc: menu.
Enter: start / restart.

Guns hold 8 shots. Throw empty guns, mugs, bottles, vases, or a frying pan. Swing held objects into enemies. Each wave takes you to a new indoor or outdoor arena. Held guns refill and stations restock; you recover one health. A downed partner returns next wave if you survive.

Play within your headset boundary. Stick movement is optional; physical dodging and turning work too.
